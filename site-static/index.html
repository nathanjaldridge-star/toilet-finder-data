💩 Toilet Finder data

Every public toilet in OpenStreetMap (amenity=toilets), plus businesses and venues tagged toilets=yes, cut into 0.5° tiles for the Toilet Finder app. Rebuilt weekly from Geofabrik extracts.

Loading counts…

  • v1/index.json: build time, counts, attribution
  • v1/tiles/<floor(lat/0.5)>_<floor(lon/0.5)>.json: one tile (404 = no toilets there). Example: Hackney
  • v1/tiles.json: list of tiles with counts and sizes
